Compliance questions, answered

What compliance cover do you hold in NSW?

NSW has no state labour hire licensing scheme, so we send the evidence unprompted: icare workers compensation, $20M public liability, ABN and Fair Work compliance, our WHS management plan and a current SafeWork NSW induction record for every worker. Ask and the pack is in your inbox the same day.

Why don't you publish your rates?

Because an honest rate depends on the classification, the site's EBA, shift pattern and duration — a table would mislead you. Tell us the crew and you get a single all-inclusive hourly rate in writing the same day, with the inclusions listed above.

How do you verify tickets and right to work?

Face-to-face interview in our Alexandria office, VEVO right-to-work check, White Card and high-risk licence verification against the SafeWork NSW register, plus reference checks with the last two supervisors. Expiries are tracked and workers are stood down before a ticket lapses.

Can a labour hire worker become permanent?

Yes. After 500 hours on your site there is no conversion fee, and casual conversion obligations are managed by us as the legal employer.
