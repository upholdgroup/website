AUDIENCE A — THE HOST

Site manager, 6am, one crew short

Job: get named, ticketed workers on site today, and know they are insured and verified before committing. Decides on responsiveness and proof, not brand.

AUDIENCE B — THE WORKER

Labourer or tradie between jobs

Job: see which Sydney suburbs have work, register from a phone in under five minutes, upload tickets from the camera roll, get paid weekly without chasing.

DESIGN CONSEQUENCE

Split the top of the page

One hero, then an immediate two-door split: Need a crew (ink card) and Looking for work (grey card). Never make one audience read the other's page.

SCOPE DECISION

Sydney only, and say so

Greater Sydney is the entire footprint. That is a strength: local branch, local crews, no interstate call centre. No rates are published anywhere — every price is quoted in writing per site.

B · TWO DOORS
The most important block on the site. Ink card = host, grey card = worker. Both visible on desktop, stacked host-first on mobile.

Six rows, first open. Each row's orange circle is the internal link to that trade page — the site's main SEO link path.

Fill time, fill rate and LTI beat generic "years of experience" — these are what a site manager is buying. No dollar figures.

Eight Greater Sydney regions, each naming its suburbs. This block, not a rates page, is the local SEO engine.

Three roles max, refreshed weekly, suburb always shown. Stale listings are the fastest way to lose worker trust.

Compliance FAQs answer the objection right before the ask. CTA offers a form and a phone number — at 6am the phone wins.

06 — RULES THIS PACKAGE COMMITS TO
NO PUBLISHED RATES
No pricing page, no rate tables, no rates on job cards. Where a number would sit, the page says what the rate includes and offers a same-day written quote. Keeps us off price comparison and out of competitors' benchmarking.
SYDNEY, EXPLICITLY
Every page states Greater Sydney. Region pages name their suburbs in body copy, not in a hidden list. No interstate claims, no city names we do not service.
PHONE IS A FIRST-CLASS CTA
Click-to-call sits beside every primary button, with the hire-desk hours next to it. Most urgent host demand converts by voice, not by form.
MOBILE FIRST, LITERALLY
Both audiences arrive on phones, often in sunlight and gloves. 44px minimum targets, high contrast, no hover-only affordances, forms that work one-handed.
