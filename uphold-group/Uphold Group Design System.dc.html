Built to
Uphold.

SYDNEY · TWO AUDIENCES · NO PUBLISHED RATES

A construction labour-hire identity system for Greater Sydney: honest black-on-white structure, one warm orange for action, full-bleed site photography, and generous rounded geometry.

Everything below is production tokens plus the component set required to build the Uphold Group site for its two audiences: builders who need a crew, and workers who need a shift.

01

Principles

01

Proof over polish

Real crews on real Sydney sites, real fill times, real names. Never illustrate what a photograph can prove, and never use stock hi-vis.

02

One loud colour

Orange means "do this next". If more than one thing on screen is orange, the page has no priority.

03

Plain words

Site managers arrive at 6am one crew short. Short sentences, trade language used correctly, timing said out loud.

04

Never a price

No rate tables, no pricing page, no rates on job cards. Where a number would sit, state what the rate includes and offer a written quote the same day.

06

UX rules & motion

Conversion path

Primary CTA reachable within one screen at all times: sticky header button on desktop, fixed bottom bar with click-to-call on mobile. The host request form is 3 steps, 2 fields each; worker registration is 2 steps with no CV and no password.

Trust order

Every page follows claim → proof → action. A claim without a fill time, ticket, suburb or named review next to it gets cut. Insurance and verification evidence sits above the fold for hosts.

Motion

160ms ease for hover, 240ms cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1) for accordion and drawers. Entrances: 12px rise + fade, once, staggered 60ms. No parallax, no autoplay carousels.

Accessibility

44px minimum targets — both audiences use phones in sunlight, often in gloves. 2px #E85F2C focus ring with 2px offset. Photo overlays get a solid gradient scrim, never opacity-reduced text. No hover-only affordances. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

DO
Two-tone lead paragraphs · full-bleed crew photo above the headline · one black card to mark the host path and the active trade · operational numbers set large · suburbs named in body copy.
DON'T
Published rate tables or dollar figures anywhere · gradient backgrounds · drop shadows on cards (use surface contrast) · orange body text · icon-only navigation · stock hi-vis photography · claims about cities we do not service.
